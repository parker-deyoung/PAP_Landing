Security and poaching patrol

We're exploring night patrols that use drones, trail cameras, and gate sensors to spot trespassers along your boundary. The illustration below shows the idea; we want to learn how you handle incidents today before building it.

  1. 1 Something crosses the line

    A boundary geofence triggered by a camera, gate sensor, or scheduled patrol

  2. 2 The drone leaves the barn

    A launch from the barn dock to the reported location

  3. 3 A thermal sweep finds them

    Thermal footage distinguishing people from nearby deer and elk

  4. 4 The poacher is tagged

    An unknown person marked on the map and tracked; checked-in guests ignored

  5. 5 You get the alert

    A phone alert with location, live view, and photos for your decision

  6. 6 It follows, then heads home

    A return to the dock, with video, photos, and GPS saved for the warden or sheriff

How we'd keep your land safe

  • Your boundary, drawn by you

    Geofences along your property lines, gates, and other key areas

  • No pattern to learn

    Changing routes and times beyond the reach of fixed cameras

  • It watches, it doesn't confront

    A drone that holds back and records, leaving the next move to you

  • A record, not a hunch

    Time, location, photos, and video ready for a warden or sheriff

  • Your herd map stays private

    Survey and security data kept in your account, never sold or shared

  • Works with what you have

    Your existing trail cameras and gate sensors as tripwires
